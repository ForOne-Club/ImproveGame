## Additions

- Added compatibility for the Fishing Machine with the MechTransfer mod. When both mods are installed, the logistics system of MT can now inject bait into the fishing machine and extract catches. When extracting catches, it automatically retains at least 100 Bass. (Thanks to [@Kadeanon](https://github.com/ForOne-Club/ImproveGame/pull/119))
- Introduced two ModCall methods: `GetFisherItems` and `SyncFisherItems`, allowing for convenient checking and updating of the fishing machine's status. (Thanks to [@Kadeanon](https://github.com/ForOne-Club/ImproveGame/pull/119))

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed entering the world after closing the mod and reloading would cause the game to crash

## Adjustments

- Improved some English localization
- Keybind reminder popup can now be permanently turned off without binding the Control Center Keybind
